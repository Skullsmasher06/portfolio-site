About

I like problems you can hold in your hands. Most of my time outside class goes to building things: modeling them in CAD, running the analysis, and then finding out on the shop floor what the model missed.

I'm most interested in mechanical design and manufacturing, especially where the two meet. A part that looks good in SolidWorks still has to be machinable, inspectable, and affordable, and I enjoy working through those tradeoffs.

Suspension upright redesign

Team project
